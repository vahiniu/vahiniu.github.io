TriageBridge

Digital health platform · AI symptom triage + telehealth · MedGemma · FHIR

A mobile health platform that answers the question everyone googles at 2 a.m. — "is this serious?" — safely, in their own language, with a real physician one tap away.

Patients face a bad choice with new symptoms: assume it's nothing (and risk delaying care) or default to the ER (costly, overcrowded). TriageBridge sits in between. You tap an interactive body map where it hurts, describe symptoms in your own words — typed or spoken, in English, हिंदी, or తెలుగు — and an AI assesses them against your complete health history. The architecture is deliberately safety-first: ~30 hard-coded red-flag rules (chest pain, stroke signs, crisis language — including Hindi/Telugu emergency phrases) run before the model and set an acuity floor the AI can raise but never lower. Anything urgent lands in a physician's queue automatically, where a doctor reviews the full picture, messages the patient, prescribes — with automatic drug-interaction screening that escalates dangerous combinations — or bumps the case to live care.

Around that core sits a full personal-health platform: trackers with longitudinal trend analysis and Apple Health import; lab reports uploaded as photos or PDFs with automatic value extraction; validated clinical risk models (FINDRISC diabetes, Framingham cardiovascular, CKD-EPI kidney) computed from the patient's own data with a "what-if" simulator — drag a slider to lose 5 kg or quit smoking and watch the real equations respond; an AI habit planner that decomposes an outcome like "lower my BP" into personalized daily habits with points, streaks, and levels; a trend-alert engine that notices a rising blood pressure before anyone thinks to look; and a medical timeline of the patient's whole story. Every encounter exports as an HL7 FHIR R4 bundle any hospital EHR can ingest.

The stack is deliberately radical: a zero-dependency Python backend (stdlib only — it runs in air-gapped clinical environments) serving a vanilla-JS PWA, with Google's open-weight MedGemma model pluggable via Ollama for fully local, private inference. No frameworks, no cloud APIs required, 88 automated tests plus a 68-check end-to-end workflow harness.
